## 4. 修正版

> "More" is a popular Christian song by Matthew West, released in 2003 from his debut album *Happy*. It reached No. 1 on the Billboard Christian Songs chart and remained at No. 1 on Radio & Records' Christian AC chart for nine consecutive weeks in 2004, making it the most-played Christian AC song of that year.

## 5. 复核风险

1. **标题歧义**："More"是常见歌曲名，多首基督教歌曲共用此标题（Matthew West、Consumed By Fire、Forest City Worship 等），声明未指定具体艺术家。
2. **"popular"的主观性**：该词无严格量化标准；Matthew West 的"More"在基督教音乐领域有客观的榜单和播放数据支撑其"热门"属性，但在主流流行音乐领域知名度有限。
3. **时间敏感性**：Matthew West 的"More"发行于 2003 年，其"热门"状态具有时效性，本报告以 2003–2004 年的榜单数据为依据。
