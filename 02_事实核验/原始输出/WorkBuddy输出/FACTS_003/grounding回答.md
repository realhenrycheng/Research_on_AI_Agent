## 2. 对用户请求的回答

- 历史上的书写文字与印刷术曾被林肯誉为推动一切发明的特殊发明，而如今GPT-4等强大生成系统正开启知识生产的新纪元，大幅提升机器生成原创内容、执行复杂任务和解决重要问题的能力，同时极大降低了人们获取其益处的门槛。原文引述："Powerful generative AI systems like GPT-4 are ushering in a new era of this technology. They're revolutionising the production of knowledge: vastly increasing the capacity of machines to generate original content, perform complex tasks and solve important problems. They are also dramatically lowering the barriers for people to access AI and its benefits."（P02）

- 这些系统也带来了严重的潜在危害，包括生成虚假信息、强化偏见与歧视、被滥用于压制性或破坏稳定目的、乃至扩散制造生物武器或实施网络攻击的知识。原文引述："These include the risk of AI generating false information, reinforcing bias and discrimination, being misused for repressive or destabilising purposes or proliferating the knowledge to make a bioweapon or conduct a cyber attack."（P03）

- 尽管存在风险，该技术仍拥有振奋人心的潜力，可用于改善生活并应对超大规模挑战——从攻克癌症、缓解气候变化到解决全球粮食不安全。其未来是使社会更公平还是更不平等、解锁突破还是沦为威权者的工具，取决于人类的选择；问题不在于是否使用它，而在于如何使用。原文引述："AI holds an exhilarating potential to improve people's lives and help solve some of the world's biggest challenges, from curing cancer to mitigating the effects of climate change to solving global food insecurity."（P04）

- 美国作为诸多领先企业、技术与人才的所在地，有能力和责任在治理方面发挥领导作用，并承诺与全球伙伴合作以确保未来体现共同价值与愿景。原文引述："The United States, as home to many of the leading companies, technologies and minds driving the AI revolution, has the ability and responsibility to lead on its governance."（P06）

- 美国已采取具体行动：发布了《人工智能权利法案蓝图》（Blueprint for an AI Bill of Rights），确立自动化系统设计与使用的原则；并制定了《人工智能风险管理框架》（AI Risk Management Framework），以加强用户保护。原文引述："We set out a Blueprint for an AI Bill of Rights with principles for how automated systems are designed and used, and developed an AI Risk Management Framework to help improve user protections."（P07）

- 拜登总统上周宣布了领先企业的一系列承诺，旨在增强安全性、保障与信任，具体包括以下准则：减轻包括滥用在内的风险；支持区分人类与机器生成内容的新技术和标准；鼓励企业与个人报告系统能力与局限；促进信息共享；推动开发应对社会最重大挑战的系统。原文引述："These commitments will mitigate risks of AI including misuse, and support new technologies and standards to distinguish between human and AI-generated content. They will encourage companies and individuals to report on systems' capabilities and limitations, and facilitate information sharing. And they will promote the development of AI systems designed to address society's greatest challenges."（P08）

- 这些承诺为限制近期风险同时促进创新提供了行动起点，将与全球伙伴的关键努力相辅相成。美国将通过日本主导的广岛进程（Hiroshima Process）与七国集团（G7）合作将其扩展和国际化，推动制定以民主价值观为指导的、面向私营行为体和政府的国际行为准则（code of conduct），以及面向各国的共同监管原则（common regulatory principles）；同时通过美欧贸易与技术委员会（US-EU Trade and Technology Council）等论坛协调各国国内方法。原文引述："G7-led action could inform an international code of conduct for private actors and governments, as well as common regulatory principles for states. As we co-ordinate globally, we will also align our domestic approaches in forums like the US-EU Trade and Technology Council."（P10）

- 美国将与其他政府密切合作建立对长期风险的共同理解及限制方法，期待参加英国举办的全球安全峰会（Global Summit on AI Safety）及其他全球参与机会以构建更安全的未来。原文引述："The US looks forward to participating in the UK's Global Summit on AI Safety and other opportunities for global engagement to build a more secure future."（P11）

- 美国致力于让发展中国家参与其中：印度将通过全球人工智能伙伴关系（Global Partnership on AI）发挥关键作用；美国亦通过联合国讨论推进包容性。原文引述："India will play a critical role, including through the Global Partnership on AI. We are also working on inclusivity for AI through discussions with the UN."（P12）

- 当前联合国可持续发展目标（Sustainable Development Goals）仅完成约12%，该技术可通过加速落实清洁用水与卫生、消除贫困、推进公共卫生及其他发展目标来改变这一轨迹。原文引述："Today, we're on track to meet just 12 per cent of the UN's Sustainable Development Goals. AI could change that trajectory by accelerating efforts to deliver clean water and sanitation, eliminate poverty, advance public health and further other development goals."（P13）

- 必须迅速且集体地行动，没有任何国家或企业能单独塑造该技术的未来。唯有通过国际社会共同的关注、智慧与协作，才能充分、安全地利用其潜力。原文引述："We must act quickly. We must also act collectively. No country or company can shape the future of AI alone."（P14）

- 本文由美国国务卿与美国商务部长联名撰写。原文引述："The writers are US secretary of state and US secretary of commerce"（P15）
