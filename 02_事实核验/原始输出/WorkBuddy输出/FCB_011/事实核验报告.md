## 2. 总体判断

待核验回答存在两项主要错误：（1）声称超大质量黑洞并合产生的引力波可以被"引力波探测器"检测——当前地面干涉仪（LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA）无法探测此类低频引力波，脉冲星计时阵列仅探测到随机背景信号而非单个并合事件；（2）声称超大质量黑洞并合"也会产生一种称为类星体的明亮闪光"——类星体是持续吸积供能的活跃星系核，并非并合直接"产生"的瞬时光爆发。其余事实基本正确。

## 4. 修正版

> When two supermassive black holes merge, the result is a single, larger black hole. The process of merging black holes is called a "black hole merger."
>
> During the merger, the black holes will release a tremendous amount of energy in the form of gravitational waves. These waves are ripples in the fabric of space-time that are created by the acceleration of massive objects. The gravitational waves from supermassive black hole mergers have extremely low frequencies (nanohertz to millihertz), which cannot be detected by current ground-based interferometers like LIGO or Virgo. Pulsar timing arrays — galaxy-scale detectors that monitor ultra-stable pulsars — have detected a stochastic gravitational wave background consistent with a population of supermassive black hole binaries, but individual merger events have not yet been resolved. The planned space-based LISA mission, scheduled for launch in the mid-2030s, is designed to detect individual supermassive black hole mergers.
>
> When two galaxies merge, the process can drive gas toward the central supermassive black holes, potentially triggering quasar activity. Quasars are some of the brightest objects in the universe and are powered by the accretion of matter onto a supermassive black hole. In gas-rich environments, a supermassive black hole merger may also produce an electromagnetic flare, though this is distinct from a quasar.
>
> It's worth noting that the merger of two supermassive black holes is a rare event, and it's likely to take place over a timescale of millions or billions of years. However, the detection of gravitational waves from individual supermassive black hole mergers would be a major scientific discovery and could provide insight into the nature of black holes and the evolution of galaxies.

## 5. 复核风险

- **C05 术语模糊性**：原文使用"gravitational wave detectors"这一泛称。PTA虽被NANOGrav宣传为"a gravitational-wave detector the size of the galaxy"，但传统意义上公众理解的"引力波探测器"多指LIGO类干涉仪。此项判断为"部分正确"而非"错误"，理由即在于此术语模糊性。
- **C06 文内矛盾**：原文C06与C08存在内在矛盾——C08正确指出类星体由吸积供能，而C06却将其描述为并合"产生"的瞬时光爆。若读者将C07-C08与C06对照，会产生困惑。
- **C09 "rare"主观性**：该表述缺乏量化依据，但在科普语境中可接受。
