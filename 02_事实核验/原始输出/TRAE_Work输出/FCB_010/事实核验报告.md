## 4. 修正版

Suggested revised answer:

Byte Pair Encoding (BPE) is commonly used for compression and for tokenization in natural language processing. It is not, by itself, an encryption scheme: replacing frequent sequences with symbols is a reversible encoding/compression process when the merge rules or substitution table are known, and it does not provide cryptographic confidentiality or randomness. Besides compression, BPE’s main benefit is subword tokenization, which helps NLP systems represent rare or unknown words with a fixed vocabulary. When used as compression, it can also reduce data size and sometimes improve transmission efficiency, but those benefits should not be confused with encryption.
