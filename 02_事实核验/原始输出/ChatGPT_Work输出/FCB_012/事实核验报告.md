## 2. 总体判断

原回答总体方向部分正确，但把“California”概括得过度统一。加州气候地域差异很大，夏季少雨和冬季较温和只适用于多数地区；纽约冬冷、夏暖湿和雨雪较多有较充分证据支持。

## 4. 修正版

California is often warmer and, in many coastal areas, milder than New York, but California's climate varies widely by region. Much of California has warm, dry summers and mild, wetter winters, while deserts can be very hot and higher elevations can be cold and snowy. New York generally has more pronounced seasons, with colder winters that bring snow and rain, and warm to hot, humid summers.

## 5. 复核风险
