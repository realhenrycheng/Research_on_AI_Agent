## 4. 修正版

Byte Pair Encoding (BPE) is a technique used for data compression and, in natural language processing, for tokenization or subword segmentation. Besides compression, BPE can help represent rare or unknown words as subword units and can improve efficiency in some NLP workflows. It does not by itself provide encryption or confidentiality: its substitutions are learned from frequency counts and do not constitute a cryptographic algorithm with a key. In compression use cases, BPE may reduce redundancy and data size, which can make storage or transmission more efficient depending on the data and overhead.

## 5. 复核风险

“BPE commonly used”属于概括性表述；本报告使用代表性论文和原始算法资料支撑。未发现可靠来源支持 BPE 本身具备加密或保密性。
